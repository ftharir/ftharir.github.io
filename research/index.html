What I research

My research sits between applied machine learning and engineering problems: keeping industrial equipment healthy (predictive maintenance), helping machines see (computer vision for quadcopters) and reading brain-imaging data (neuroimaging).

The way of working is the same in all three: implement the model, design the experiment, evaluate it carefully, and write it up.

Publication

From Predictive Maintenance to Prescriptive: A Survey of Retrieval-Augmented Generation-Driven Systems for End-to-End Maintenance Decision-Making

B. H. Khalaj, M. Rohban, F. Harirforoush, et al.

Preprint on SSRN, 2026. Submitted to a Q1 journal, under review.

SSRN preprint (DOI 10.2139/ssrn.7180409) ResearchGate

About the survey

Predictive maintenance asks when a machine will fail. Prescriptive maintenance goes one step further and asks what to do about it. The survey looks at how retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), where a language model answers using documents it retrieves, can connect the two into end-to-end maintenance decision-making.

It is a preprint on SSRN and has been submitted to a Q1 journal, where it is under review.

Methods I worked with, in short

Plain-language background for the methods named above; these are explanations, not results.

Transformers

Neural networks built on attention, which let a model weigh every part of a sequence against every other part. Used in the battery and bearing health-monitoring research.

Contrastive learning

Learning representations by pulling similar samples together and pushing different ones apart, so useful features can be learned with few labels.

Physics-informed neural networks

Networks trained with the equations of the physical system as part of the loss, so predictions stay consistent with physics.

Diffusion models

Generative models that learn by removing noise step by step. The same denoising idea underlies the PVD model from the molecular task.

Graph neural networks

Networks that work on graphs, such as brain regions linked by functional connectivity, and learn from both nodes and links.

Object detection and tracking

Finding objects in each frame and following them over time, in real time, from a moving quadcopter camera.
